What it costs

This is an estimate built from the answers, not a measurement. In this example, about 6 hours a week go to rebuilding context: finding the latest brief, re-asking the client, and re-explaining decisions. At 48 working weeks, that's about 288 hours a year.

What to change first

  1. Put the brief and the reasons behind it in one record the whole team can open at kickoff.
  2. Name one person who owns each handoff from account to production.
  3. After each round of client feedback, write down what changed and why, in the same record.
